_Your daily dose of infosec chaos_

---

Schools are having a rough week: a UK school estate got ransomed by a crew that may have let an AI model drive parts of the intrusion, and a US education vendor leaked data on over a million people. Meanwhile the patch queue is stacked - a 9.8-rated Linux TCP bug, a Node-RED flaw under live attack, and 200-plus Android fixes. Grab coffee and open your ticketing system.

### 3AM Ransomware Hits UK Schools With A Little Help From AI

A 3AM affiliate slipped in through an unmanaged host and encrypted around 37 of the nearly 50 machines it reached across a UK school estate.
Researchers spotted commands being written and rewritten mid-attack, a hallmark of a model improvising on the job - not fully autonomous hacking, but close enough to be uncomfortable.

**What to do:** Hunt down unmanaged hosts on your network and segment aggressively - one forgotten machine was the entire foothold here.

---

### Red Hat Ships Fix For 9.8-Rated Linux TCP RCE

A use-after-free in the kernel's IPv4 TCP stack gives remote attackers a shot at code execution, and patched kernels are now out for Red Hat Enterprise Linux 8, 9, and 10.
Ubuntu users are less lucky: fixes for the three newest LTS releases are still stuck in the opt-in testing channel with no date for a regular update.

**What to do:** Patch your RHEL fleet now; on Ubuntu, pull from the testing pocket or mitigate - do not sit around waiting for the regular update.

---

### Node-RED Flaw Under Active Attack Right Now

Attackers are hammering a critical Node-RED vulnerability from a single IP, dropping Linux bots and webshells on exposed instances.
Node-RED runs the automation flows in a lot of labs, homes, and industrial setups, which makes it a nice quiet pivot point - and "quiet" is exactly what a webshell wants.

**What to do:** Update Node-RED immediately and make sure its editor UI is not reachable from the public internet.

---

### Android November Update Squashes Over 200 Flaws

Google's November 2026 patch batch fixes more than 200 vulnerabilities, including a critical Framework bug that gives remote code execution with zero user interaction.
No tap, no link, no unlucky click - just a device that answers the phone at the wrong time. Everyone's favorite kind of vulnerability.

**What to do:** Apply the November security update as soon as your OEM ships it, and check that your MDM actually covers every managed device.

---

### Frontline Education Breach Impacts Over 1.3 Million

Attackers exploited a flaw in third-party software to steal personal information on school district staff and students from education vendor Frontline.
Education suppliers keep getting popped, and the loot is exactly the kind of PII - kids, teachers, staff - that powers spear-phishing and identity fraud for years.

**What to do:** If your district uses Frontline, assume the PII is exposed - warn staff about targeted phishing and consider credit freezes for affected families.
